⚖️ Balanced-Focus System

Many students over-focus on their favorite subject while accidentally neglecting other critical courses until the night before the exam.

Proximo calculates your Balance Score (0-100) by comparing your logged study hours against each course's credit weight:

  • 85 - 100 (Harmonious Orbit): Effort is evenly distributed across all courses.
  • 70 - 84 (Healthy Spread): Minor variation, but all courses are receiving attention.
  • 55 - 69 (Orbital Wobble): One subject is receiving excessive time while others lag behind.
  • Below 55 (Gravitational Drift): High risk of falling behind in neglected courses.

💡 Pro-Tip: Log a quick 25-minute Pomodoro in a neglected subject to immediately boost your balance score!
